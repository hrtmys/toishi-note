# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project — filing issues, opening pull requests, joining discussion — to be a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what's best for the project and its users.
- Show empathy toward other contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal/political attacks, public or private.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Trolling, deliberately inflammatory comments, or sustained disruption of discussion.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the project in public spaces elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening a private security advisory (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) or contacting the maintainer directly. All reports will be reviewed and investigated, and responded to as appropriate to the circumstances. The maintainer is obligated to maintain confidentiality regarding the reporter of an incident.

Project maintainers who don't follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other members of the project's leadership.
